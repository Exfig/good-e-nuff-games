Good-E-Nuff Games

Good-E-Nuff Games is a YouTube channel of themed roundups of free indie games and Steam demos, each ending with a verdict: is it Good-E-Nuff? Every episode also asks viewers to guess which games were made with help from AI.

Watch on YouTube: @good-e-nuffgames

Good-E-Nuff Games uploader

The Good-E-Nuff Games uploader is a private desktop tool that the channel's owner runs on their own PC. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own finished episodes and Shorts to the channel, with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and to read back each upload's processing status. It is not offered to anyone else, and it only ever signs in to the Good-E-Nuff Games channel.
